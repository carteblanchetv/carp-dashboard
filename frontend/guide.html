Section 1

Signing In & Password Recovery

Welcome to the new CARP Dashboard. To get started, you will need to access the site and authenticate using the supplied login credentials.

cb-deliverables.web.app/login
1
Open your browser and navigate to cb-deliverables.web.app.
2
Under Work Email and Password, enter your credentials set up by your administrator, then click Sign In.
💡
Account Setup: Your account must first be whitelisted and created by the administrator. If you are unable to log in, please contact the Admin to ensure your profile has been added.

Forgotten Password Recovery

If you have forgotten your password, you can reset it securely using the self-service reset flow:

1
Click the Forgot Password? link positioned right above the Password input.
2
Enter your username, and the email address you need the Reset Link to be sent to. NOTE: these two adresses do not have to be the same.
3
Check your email inbox (and spam folder) for a message containing a secure link. Click the link to define a new password.
Section 2

Navigating Your Dashboard

Upon logging in, you will land on the central Forms Dashboard. This page displays the forms available to you and serves as the hub for tracking your active stories.

cb-deliverables.web.app/index
1
Action Cards: The grid at the top contains cards to perform actions, such as starting a New Proposal, managing your credentials in My Account, or launching the Submit Invoice portal.
2
Global Search: The search bar allows you to quickly filter story titles, summaries, and expert names. You can also type in another producer's name to find their profile and view their stories.
⚠️
IMPORTANT! Stories marked as Sensitive will not show in the search results.
3
My Stories Table: Scroll down to view the progress of all the proposals you have submitted. The table includes columns for Status, Commission Number, Delivery Date, and Actions (Edit / View / Delete).

Updating your Profile Info and Credentials

You can update your first name, last name and password here. The Admin will be notified when you make changes in this section.

cb-deliverables.web.app/profile

Tracking Your Proposals

Use the My Stories list on your dashboard to monitor the status of your submissions. Here is a glossary of the status badges you will see:

cb-deliverables.web.app/index
Pending
Under Editorial Review: Your proposal has been submitted successfully and is waiting for the editorial team to review and commission.
Commissioned
Story Approved: The editorial team has approved your story. A commission number has been assigned, and the deliverables phase is unlocked.
Rejected
Not Proceeding: The story proposal was not selected for this production cycle. You can still view the details but editing is disabled.
Draft
Work In Progress: You have started filling in the proposal, but have not yet clicked submit. Only you can see this draft.
Section 3

Story Proposal Submissions

This is where you create your story proposals. The editorial team will then review and commission your stories once submitted.

cb-deliverables.web.app/proposal
1
Click the New Proposal card from the Dashboard.
2
Fill in all required details, such as the Story Title, One-Liner summary, and the main Story Summary.
3
Scroll down to enter expert contacts, key interviewees, and production angles. NOTE: You can leave these fields empty if you're unsure about Case Studies, Experts, etc. You can edit your proposal at a later stage.
4
Press Submit Proposal at the bottom of the page when complete.
💾
Autosave Integration: Your changes are automatically saved to the database in real-time as you type. If you close your browser or lose connection, your drafts are safely stored and can be edited via the dashboard anytime.
⚠️
Required Fields: Fields outlined in red are mandatory. The form will prevent submission and highlight any incomplete fields.

Editing Draft or Pending Stories

If your proposal is in DRAFT or PENDING status, you can modify it at any time. Simply find it in the dashboard table and click the ✏️ Edit button. Once a story is COMMISSIONED, it becomes read-only to lock the editorial mandate, but you can upload deliverables for it.

Section 4

Insert Details & Crew Assignment

Once the editorial team reviews and approves your proposal, they change the story status to COMMISSIONED. This status change automatically unlocks the Insert Details section on the proposal form, allowing you to assign the production crew who will work on the story.

cb-deliverables.web.app/proposal?id=...
1
Go to the Dashboard, find your commissioned story (status badge is green: Commissioned), and click the ✏️ Edit button.
2
Scroll down below Additional Info. The previously locked Insert Details panel will now be fully visible and interactive.
3
Fill in or select the assigned crew members (NOTE: Not all fields need to be completed):
  • Presenter: Select the presenter from the dropdown menu (e.g. Masa Kekana, Macfarlane Moleli) or select "Other" to specify a custom name.
  • Researcher(s): Enter the names of the Researcher if applicable.
  • Camera & Camera Assistant: List the camera operators used on the shoot and enter the names of the Camera Assistant if applicable.
  • DOP (Director of Photography): Specify the name and surname of your DOP.
  • Sound: Enter the names of the Sound Engineer.
  • Offline/Online Editors: Enter the names of the Offline and Online Editors.
  • AFM (Audio Final Mix) crew: Specify the name and surname of the Audio Final Mix engineer.
  • Additional Crew: Enter the names of any additional crew.
4
Click the Save button (or rely on the auto-save status) to ensure the crew list is logged in the database.
💡
Automatic Form Unlocking: You cannot access or edit the Insert Details section while a proposal is in Draft or Pending status. This section is reserved exclusively for approved, commissioned stories.
Section 5

Call Sheet Management

Call Sheets can be created, edited, and submitted once a proposal has been Commissioned. The Call Sheet allows you to register shoot dates, crew roles, security info, risk assessments, movement orders, equipment setups, and travel/accommodation details.

cb-deliverables.web.app/proposal?id=...
1
Go to the Dashboard, find your commissioned story, and click the ✏️ Edit button.
2
Click on the Call Sheet button at the top of the page. You will then be taken to an empty call sheet where you need to add crew details, equipment info, and itinerary.

NB! The Call Sheet MUST be completed before your shoot. Without it, insurance won't cover any damages.
⚠️
Required Fields for Submission: When submitting the Call Sheet, the following fields are strictly required:
  • Shoot Dates: You must enter and specify your shoot dates.
  • Key Crew: You must complete the Name, Surname, and Phone details for the Presenter, Producer, and DOP.
  • Risk Assessment: The Risk Assessment description field must be filled in.
Section 6

Submitting Footage Declarations

Once you've delivered your insert, you need to submit a Footage Declaration. You can add the footage manually, or import it via Excel or Word documents.

cb-deliverables.web.app/proposal?id=...&view=preview
1
Go to your Dashboard, find your commissioned story, and click 📄 View (or click the story title).
2
On the story details page, locate the Story Deliverables menu bar at the top, and click the ORANGE Footage Declaration button.
3
The system opens the declaration sheet, automatically mapping the commission number and details.
4
Click Add Row to input footage items. Specify the Type (e.g., Image, News Article, Video), the Source (e.g. YouTube link or Getty), and the exact durations.
5
Click Submit Declaration to save it. You can access it and edit it as needed before broadcast.
Section 7

Final Script Submission

Once VO has been done, you must submit the final script matching the final edit.

cb-deliverables.web.app/proposal?id=...
⚠️
IMPORTANT! Do NOT include anonymous source names in the script. Also ensure all subtitles are exactly the same as they appear in the final mix - this includes punctuation and line breaks.
1
On the details page for your commissioned story, click the Final Script button in the purple deliverables bar.
2
The script editor will load. You can paste your script text or compile it directly in the editor.
3
Enter the duration, voiceover scripts, visual descriptors, and audio tracks aligned with timecodes.
4
Save the script. When finalised, click Submit Final Script to lock it for review.
⚠️
IMPORTANT! This is a required deliverable. Payments will not be processed before the Final Script has been submitted.
