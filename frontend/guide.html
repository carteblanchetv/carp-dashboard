1

Story Proposal Submissions

This is where you create your story proposals. The editorial team will then review and commission your stories once submitted.

cb-deliverables.web.app/proposal

Click the New Proposal card from the Dashboard.
2
Fill in all required details, such as the Story Title, One-Liner summary, and the main Story Summary.

⚠️
Required Fields: Fields outlined in red are mandatory. The form will prevent submission and highlight any incomplete fields.

If your proposal is in DRAFT or PENDING status, you can modify it at any time. Simply find it in the dashboard table and click the ✏️ Edit button. Once a story is COMMISSIONED, it becomes read-only to lock the editorial mandate, but you can upload deliverables for it.

Section 4

Insert Details & Crew Assignment

Once the editorial team reviews and approves your proposal, they change the story status to COMMISSIONED. This status change automatically unlocks the Insert Details section on the proposal form, allowing you to assign the production crew who will work on the story.

cb-deliverables.web.app/proposal?id=...
1
Go to the Dashboard, find your commissioned story (status badge is green: Commissioned), and click the ✏️ Edit button.
2
Scroll down below Additional Info. The previously locked Insert Details panel will now be fully visible and interactive.
3
Fill in or select the assigned crew members (NOTE: Not all fields need to be completed):
  • Presenter: Select the presenter from the dropdown menu (e.g. Masa Kekana, Macfarlane Moleli) or select "Other" to specify a custom name.
  • Researcher(s): Enter the names of the Researcher if applicable.
  • Camera & Camera Assistant: List the camera operators used on the shoot and enter the names of the Camera Assistant if applicable.
  • DOP (Director of Photography): Specify the name and surname of your DOP.
  • Sound: Enter the names of the Sound Engineer.
  • Offline/Online Editors: Enter the names of the Offline and Online Editors.
  • AFM (Audio Final Mix) crew: Specify the name and surname of the Audio Final Mix engineer.
  • Additional Crew: Enter the names of any additional crew.
4
Click the Save button (or rely on the auto-save status) to ensure the crew list is logged in the database.
💡
Automatic Form Unlocking: You cannot access or edit the Insert Details section while a proposal is in Draft or Pending status. This section is reserved exclusively for approved, commissioned stories.

Call Sheet Management

Call Sheets can be created, edited, and submitted once a proposal has been Commissioned. The Call Sheet allows you to register shoot dates, crew roles, security info, risk assessments, movement orders, equipment setups, and travel/accommodation details.

cb-deliverables.web.app/proposal?id=...
cb-deliverables.web.app/proposal?id=...#CallSheet
1
Go to the Dashboard, find your commissioned story, and click the ✏️ Edit button.
2

NB! The Call Sheet MUST be completed before your shoot. Without it, insurance won't cover any damages.
⚠️
Required Fields for Submission: When submitting the Call Sheet, the following fields are strictly required:
  • Shoot Dates: You must enter and specify your shoot dates.
  • Crew Details: All crew members added/listed in the Crew table must have their complete **First Name**, **Surname**, and **Phone Number** filled in.
  • Physical Address: You must specify the physical address for all locations you are shooting in (located under the Movement Orders / Location section).
  • Risk Assessment: The Risk Assessment description field must be filled in.

Equipment - As Per Dop Button

Under the Equipment section, you can check the box for As Per the DOP. Selecting this option merges all separate equipment sub-sections (Camera, Audio, Lenses, Lighting, Rigs, and Other Equipment) into a single unified section labeled "As Per the DOP and supporting crew". This simplifies entry and indicates that details are managed directly by the DOP and crew.

As Per DOP Button: Off (Default detailed list view)

Section 8

Viewer Submissions

The Viewer Submissions is where you'll find story submissions and leads sent to us via the My Story inbox, or the Carte Blanche web submissions page.

Page Layout & The Three Tables

The page is divided into three sections, each representing a different stage in the submission workflow:

1
Viewer Submissions (Inbox): All new, unactioned messages from the public appear here. This is your main inbox — sorted by date received, newest first.
2
Under Investigation: Submissions you have flagged as useful are automatically moved to this table. This signals to the whole team that someone is actively looking into the story lead.
3
Resolved Submissions: Leads that have been fully actioned and closed appear here as a record. These are read-only.

Opening a Submission

Click the 📄 View button in the Actions column of any row to open the full submission in a modal. The modal displays the sender's details, the full message body, and any attachments.

🔄 Grouped & Nested Submissions

To prevent clutter and keep your inbox organised, the system automatically groups submissions sent by the same individual.

💡
How Grouping Works:
  • Email-Based Grouping: Multiple submissions from the same email address are combined into a single entry if they are sent within 3 months (90 days) of each other. If 3 months have passed since their last message, any new email from them will be treated as a new, separate submission.
  • Count Badges: In the tables, grouped rows display a count badge next to the sender's name (for example, Gerhard Botha (4)), showing you how many submissions are nested inside.
  • Using Tab Navigation: When you open a grouped submission, a row of date and time tabs will appear at the top of the modal (e.g., 13 Jun 16:10). Click on any tab to view that specific message's contents, attachments, and options.
Submission detail modal

⭐ Mark as Useful

Found a lead worth investigating? Click the ⭐ Mark as Useful button at the bottom of the submission modal.

1
The submission is immediately removed from the main Inbox and moved to the Under Investigation table below.
2
Your name is recorded in the Actioned By column of the Under Investigation table, so colleagues can see who is handling the lead.
3
You can undo this action at any time: open the submission again from the Under Investigation table and click ⭐ Remove from Useful — it will return to the main inbox.

✉️ Contact Individual

Inside the submission modal, click the ✉️ Contact Individual button. This opens your default email client with a new message pre-addressed to the viewer's email address and the original subject pre-filled. Simply type your reply and send — no need to copy and paste addresses.

💡
Note: The Contact Individual button uses your computer's installed email application (e.g. Outlook or Apple Mail). Make sure your email client is configured on your device for this to work.

🚨 Report as Spam

If a submission is clearly junk, a scam, or unwanted marketing, click the 🚨 Report as Spam button in the modal.

1
A confirmation prompt will appear. Click OK to confirm — or Cancel if you changed your mind.
2
The submission is flagged and an automated notification is sent to the Admin to review and permanently block the sender's address.
⚠️
Use with care: Reporting as spam is a permanent action that triggers a block request. Only use it for clearly unsolicited or malicious messages — not for legitimate tips you simply aren't interested in pursuing.
Section 9

Forms Hub: Release Forms & Production Agreements

The Forms Hub is the dedicated central repository for all Carte Blanche legal release forms, contributor permissions, licensing agreements, and production templates. Whether you are on shoot in the field or working from your desk, the Forms Hub ensures all required legal documents can be completed quickly, securely, and seamlessly.

You can access the hub directly from the main Dashboard by clicking the Legal & Production Forms card, or via the Forms Hub & Blank PDFs button in the Quick Links toolbar.

Two Ways to Complete Forms: Fill Online & Blank PDF

Every agreement in the Forms Hub gives producers two flexible completion options tailored to different production environments:

📝 Fill Online

Paperless • In-Browser

Complete and digitally sign agreements directly in any web browser on desktop, tablet, or smartphone without installing extra software.

  • ✍️ Digital Signatures: Contributors can sign directly on-screen using a touch screen, mouse, or typed verified signature.
  • 📬 Automatic Delivery to Legal: Submitting a form automatically routes the completed PDF to release@carteblanche.co.za.
  • 📧 Producer Email Copy: The producer automatically receives a copy of the completed form via email for their records.
  • ⚡ Instant Compliance: No printing, physical scanning, or manual attachment emailing required.

📥 Blank PDF

Offline • WhatsApp Ready

Download neat, fillable PDF documents with interactive form fields designed for mobile and desktop readers.

  • 💬 Send via WhatsApp or Email: Easily send the blank form directly to your case studies, sources, or contributors to complete on their devices.
  • 📶 100% Offline Completion: Forms can be opened, filled out, and signed even while completely offline without an active internet connection.
  • 📱 Save on Phone Anytime: If you anticipate connectivity issues, you can save a blank version of the document on your phone to be accessed anytime.
  • 📍 Shoot-Ready: Perfect for deep-field investigations, rural locations, basements, and areas experiencing network outages.
📬
Automatic Email Routing & Confirmation: When they submit a form, it will go to release@carteblanche.co.za and the producer will receive a copy of the completed form via email.
📱
Offline & WhatsApp Workflow: When downloading a blank form, you can easily send it to your case studies or sources via WhatsApp or email to be completed, even while offline. If you anticipate connectivity issues, you can save a blank version of the document on your phone to be accessed anytime.

Workflow 1: Filling & Submitting Online

Follow these steps when you or your contributor have an internet connection and want immediate paperless filing:

1
Open the Forms Hub from your dashboard and locate the required form (e.g. Participant Release Form, Minor Release Form, or Location Agreement).
2
Click the blue 📝 Fill Online button on the form card.
3
Enter the participant or location details (Full Name, ID/Passport Number, Phone Number, Email Address, and Story Name).
4
Have the interviewee provide their signature using the digital signature pad. They can draw using their finger or mouse, or click the Type tab to type their signature.
5
Click Submit. The system automatically compiles the official PDF, transmits it directly to release@carteblanche.co.za, and sends a confirmation copy to your producer email inbox.

Workflow 2: Sharing Online Forms with Participants (WhatsApp, Email & QR Code)

Producers can generate and share online forms directly with interviewees, parents, or property owners so they can complete and digitally sign on their own mobile phone or computer — no account or staff login required:

1
On any form card in the Forms Hub (or via the 🔗 Share with Participant button at the top of an online form), click Share Online Form with Participant.
2
In the sharing popup, enter the Story Title, optional Commission #, your Producer Name, and your Producer Email (where you will receive a signed copy). You can also type the participant's name to pre-fill it for them.
3
Choose your preferred sharing channel:
  • WhatsApp: Launches WhatsApp with a polite pre-written message and direct link.
  • Copy Link: Copies the instant-access link to your clipboard.
  • Email: Opens a pre-addressed email draft in your default email client.
  • QR Code: Displays an on-screen QR code for the contributor to scan instantly with their phone camera on shoot.
4
The participant opens the link directly on their mobile device or desktop without having to sign in. They see an official Carte Blanche invitation banner, review the pre-filled story info, draw or type their signature, and submit.
5
Upon submission, the completed PDF is instantly transmitted to release@carteblanche.co.za, sent to the producer's inbox, and sent to the participant's email.

Workflow 3: Downloading & Using Blank PDFs Offline

Follow these steps when filming on location, sending paperwork to sources in advance, or working in areas with weak cellular reception:

1
On any form card in the Forms Hub, click the 📥 Blank PDF button to download the fillable interactive PDF template.
2
Save in advance: If you anticipate connectivity issues or are heading to a remote location, save a blank version of the document directly on your phone's storage or Files app before leaving so it can be accessed anytime.
3
Share with sources: You can easily send the blank form to your case studies or sources via WhatsApp or email to be completed, even while offline.
4
Complete offline: The contributor fills in their particulars and signs the interactive document directly in their mobile PDF reader (such as Adobe Acrobat or Apple Books) without requiring an internet connection.
5
Once you or your source regain network connectivity, forward the signed PDF to release@carteblanche.co.za and keep a copy in your story folder.
